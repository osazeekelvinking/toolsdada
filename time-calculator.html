How Time Calculations Work

Time calculations operate on a sexagesimal (base-60) numerical system for minutes and seconds, combined with a 24-hour cycle for days. When performing time arithmetic, all values are normalized to fundamental base seconds, manipulated algebraically, and then redistributed across days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

1. Base Conversion Formulas

  • 1 Minute = 60 Seconds
  • 1 Hour = 60 Minutes = 3,600 Seconds
  • 1 Day = 24 Hours = 1,440 Minutes = 86,400 Seconds
  • Decimal Hours = \(\text{Hours} + \frac{\text{Minutes}}{60} + \frac{\text{Seconds}}{3600}\)

2. Normalization Algorithm

Given total seconds \(S_{\text{total}}\):

  1. \(\text{Days} = \lfloor S_{\text{total}} / 86400 \rfloor\)
  2. \(\text{Hours} = \lfloor (S_{\text{total}} \pmod{86400}) / 3600 \rfloor\)
  3. \(\text{Minutes} = \lfloor (S_{\text{total}} \pmod{3600}) / 60 \rfloor\)
  4. \(\text{Seconds} = S_{\text{total}} \pmod{60}\)
