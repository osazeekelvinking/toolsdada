How Duration Between Times is Calculated

A duration calculator determines the precise physical time elapsed between a starting moment \(T_1\) and an ending moment \(T_2\). When times cross midnight, 24 hours (1,440 minutes or 86,400 seconds) are added to account for the diurnal cycle rollover.

1. Decimal Hours Conversion

In payroll, aviation, and scientific scheduling, minutes and seconds are often converted to decimal fractional hours:

\[ \text{Decimal Hours} = \text{Hours} + \frac{\text{Minutes}}{60} + \frac{\text{Seconds}}{3600} \]

For example, 7 hours and 45 minutes equals \(7 + \frac{45}{60} = 7.75\) decimal hours.

2. Midnight Rollover Logic

If the end time is numerically earlier than the start time (e.g. 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM) and overnight mode is active, the calculation evaluates:

\[ \Delta T = (T_{\text{end}} + 24\text{ hours}) - T_{\text{start}} \]
