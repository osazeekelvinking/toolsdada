How Grade Point Average (GPA) is Calculated

Grade Point Average represents the mathematical average of all course grades weighted by their respective academic credit hours. Each letter grade corresponds to a standard numerical quality point on a 4.0 scale:

2. The GPA Formula

\[ \text{GPA} = \frac{\sum (\text{Course Grade Points} \times \text{Credit Hours})}{\sum \text{Credit Hours}} \]

For example, if you earn an A (4.0) in a 4-credit biology class (16 points) and a B (3.0) in a 3-credit history class (9 points), your GPA is \(\frac{16 + 9}{4 + 3} = \frac{25}{7} = 3.57\).
