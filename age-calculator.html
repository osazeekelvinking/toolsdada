Chronological Age Arithmetic and Calendar Complexities

Calculating your precise age sounds straightforward, but Gregorian calendar irregularities make exact arithmetic surprisingly tricky. Months range between 28, 29, 30, and 31 days, and every four years a leap year introduces a 366th day. Simply dividing the total days lived by 365 or 365.25 produces inaccuracies when evaluating exact legal age milestones.

How Gregorian Date Difference Arithmetic Works

To determine chronological age accurately:

  • First, subtract the birth day from the target date's day. If the target day is smaller, borrow days from the preceding calendar month.
  • Next, subtract the birth month from the target month. If the target month is smaller, borrow 12 months from the target year.
  • Finally, subtract the birth year from the target year.
Milestone Perspective: What Does 10,000 Days Mean?
