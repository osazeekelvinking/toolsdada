Understanding Global Time Zones & UTC

The Earth rotates 360 degrees in approximately 24 hours, meaning it advances by 15 degrees of longitude every hour. Standard time zones are established relative to Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), which is maintained by atomic clocks at the Prime Meridian in Greenwich, London.

1. Daylight Saving Time (DST)

Many regions in North America and Europe advance clocks forward by 1 hour during spring (Daylight Saving Time) and back 1 hour in autumn (Standard Time). Consequently, the offset difference between two cities can shift by an hour depending on whether either or both locations observe DST at that specific time of the year.

2. Meeting Planner Status Legend

  • 🟢 Business Hours (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM): Ideal for live business calls and client collaboration.
  • 🟡 Extended Waking Hours (7:00 AM – 9:00 AM, 5:00 PM – 11:00 PM): Acceptable for urgent calls or personal communication.
  • 🔴 Sleeping Hours (11:00 PM – 7:00 AM): Unsuitable for real-time contact.
