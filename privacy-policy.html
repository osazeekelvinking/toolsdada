Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Client-Side Privacy

At ToolsBuddy (accessible at https://toolsbuddy.pages.dev), we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Unlike traditional web utilities that transmit your text, code, financial inputs, and personal dates to backend database clusters, ToolsBuddy tools operate 100% client-side in your web browser.

2. Data Collection and Processing

No Personal Data Uploads: When you use our calculators (Percentage, Mortgage, Age, BMI), text tools (Case Converter, Word Counter, Line Deduplicator), or developer tools (JSON Formatter, QR Code Studio, WhatsApp Link Generator), your data is executed purely in local RAM memory via JavaScript.

Local Storage: Certain tools may save non-identifiable draft preferences (such as light/dark theme selection or an active text draft) directly in your browser's private localStorage container. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache.

3. Advertising Partners & Google AdSense

ToolsBuddy is monetized through third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to ToolsBuddy and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Interaction-Driven Monetization Model

In accordance with Google AdSense Policies, advertisements on ToolsBuddy are not configured with unauthorized background automatic timers. Ad impressions are requested cleanly during user page visits and user-initiated tool interactions (such as pressing Calculate, Convert, or Format). Unfilled advertisement units automatically collapse to preserve a clean reading environment.

5. Log Files and Analytics

Like standard web hosts (such as Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages), our hosting providers may record basic network server log files (including browser user agents, referring/exit pages, and access timestamps) for standard DDoS mitigation, security maintenance, and traffic bandwidth analysis.

6. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices or security architecture, please connect with our parent educational network at SabiMentors.
