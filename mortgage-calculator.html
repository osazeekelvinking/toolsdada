How the Mortgage Calculator Works

A comprehensive mortgage payment consists of more than just repaying the borrowed principal. Most lenders require an escrow account covering recurring municipal taxes and homeowner hazards. The standard formula used to compute your fixed monthly Principal & Interest (P&I) is:

M = P × [ r(1 + r)n ] ÷ [ (1 + r)n - 1 ]

Where M is your monthly P&I payment, P is your loan principal amount, r is your monthly interest rate (annual APR ÷ 12), and n is the total number of monthly payments (years × 12).

Understanding the PITI Components

  • Principal: The portion of each payment that directly decreases the outstanding loan balance.
  • Interest: The fee charged by the lender for borrowing capital, front-loaded during the early years of the mortgage.
  • Property Taxes: Annual taxes levied by your local municipal county or school district, divided into 12 monthly escrow installments.
  • Homeowners Insurance: Hazard insurance policy protecting the physical property against storm, fire, or catastrophic damage.
  • HOA Dues: Homeowners Association assessment for common amenities, maintenance, and community upkeep.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a PITI mortgage payment?

PITI stands for Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance. It represents the total monthly cost of homeownership beyond the base loan repayment.

How does down payment affect my monthly payment?

A larger down payment lowers your principal loan balance, reduces total interest paid, and allows you to avoid Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) if you put down at least 20%.

How does extra monthly payment save money on a mortgage?

Extra monthly payments go straight to the principal balance, reducing compounding interest for all subsequent months and shortening your loan term by years.

Can I use this calculator for non-US currencies?

Yes! Use the Currency selector at the top to compute in EUR (€), GBP (£), NGN (₦), INR (₹), CAD (C$), AUD (A$), or JPY (¥).
