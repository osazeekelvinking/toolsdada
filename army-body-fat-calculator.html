The Army Body Composition Program (AR 600-9)

The primary objective of the Army Body Composition Program (ABCP) is to ensure all soldiers maintain optimal physical readiness, operational discipline, and cardiovascular health. Soldiers whose body weight exceeds the screening table height-weight benchmarks are required to undergo the official circumference tape test.

Official AR 600-9 Taping Formulas

Measurements are taken three times consecutively and averaged to the nearest 0.5 inch (or converted to cm):

Male (DA Form 5500):
% Body Fat = 86.010 × log10(Abdomen − Neck) − 70.041 × log10(Height) + 36.76

Female (DA Form 5501):
% Body Fat = 163.205 × log10(Waist + Hip − Neck) − 97.684 × log10(Height) − 78.387

Per AR 600-9 paragraph 3-3, all fractions are rounded down to the nearest whole percentage point.

The ACFT 540+ Exemption Policy

In recognition of muscular strength and high physical conditioning, the US Department of the Army instituted an exemption policy: any soldier who scores 540 or higher on the Army Combat Fitness Test (ACFT), with a minimum of 80 points in every individual event, is completely exempt from the body fat tape test regardless of height or scale weight.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum allowable body fat percentage in the US Army?

Under Army Regulation AR 600-9, maximum allowable body fat standards depend on age. For males: Age 17–20 is 20%, 21–27 is 22%, 28–39 is 24%, and 40+ is 26%. For females: Age 17–20 is 30%, 21–27 is 32%, 28–39 is 34%, and 40+ is 36%.

What is the Army ACFT tape test exemption?

Under updated Army Directive policy, soldiers who score 540 points or higher on the Army Combat Fitness Test (ACFT), with at least 80 points in each of the six events, are completely exempt from the body fat tape test regardless of their weight on the scale.

How does the Army round body fat percentages?

In accordance with AR 600-9, all body fat calculations are rounded down to the nearest whole integer. For example, a calculated 22.9% is officially recorded as 22% on DA Form 5500.

Where are the official measurement tape sites located?

For male soldiers: The neck circumference is measured just below the larynx, and the abdomen circumference is measured horizontally at the midpoint of the navel. For female soldiers: The neck is measured below the larynx, the waist is measured at the narrowest point of the torso, and the hips are measured at the widest protrusion of the buttocks.
