Solving Triangles with Trigonometry

Solving a triangle means finding the lengths of all three sides and the measurements of all three interior angles. In Euclidean planar geometry, the sum of all interior angles always equals 180 degrees (π radians):

α + β + γ = 180°

1. The Law of Cosines

When three sides are known (SSS) or two sides and the included angle are known (SAS), the Law of Cosines relates all three sides to any single angle:

c2 = a2 + b2 − 2ab × cos(γ)
cos(α) = (b2 + c2 − a2) / (2bc)

2. The Law of Sines

When two angles and one side are known (ASA or AAS), the Law of Sines provides direct proportions between each side and the sine of its opposite angle:

a / sin(α) = b / sin(β) = c / sin(γ) = 2R

Where R is the circumradius of the triangle's circumscribed circle.

3. Heron's Formula for Area

Given all three side lengths, the area is found without calculating angles using Heron's formula:

s = (a + b + c) / 2
Area = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)]

Frequently Asked Questions

What information is needed to solve a triangle?

To solve a triangle, you must know at least three parts, with at least one being a side length. Standard combinations include: SSS (three sides), SAS (two sides and included angle), ASA (two angles and included side), or AAS (two angles and non-included side).

What is the Triangle Inequality Theorem?

The Triangle Inequality Theorem states that for any valid triangle, the sum of any two side lengths must always be strictly greater than the length of the remaining third side (a + b > c, a + c > b, b + c > a).

How is the area of a triangle calculated from 3 sides (Heron's Formula)?

Heron's Formula calculates area using the semi-perimeter s = (a + b + c) / 2: Area = sqrt[s * (s - a) * (s - b) * (s - c)].

What is the ambiguous case (SSA)?

The ambiguous case occurs when two sides and a non-included angle are known (Side-Side-Angle). Depending on the relative lengths and height, SSA may produce zero triangles (impossible geometry), exactly one unique triangle, or two distinct triangles.
