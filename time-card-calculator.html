How Time Cards and Overtime Pay Work

A time card calculator tracks hourly wages by summing daily work durations, deducting non-compensable meal breaks, and applying appropriate statutory overtime multipliers.

1. Calculating Shift Hours

For each day, the net paid hours are determined by:

\[ \text{Daily Hours} = \frac{(\text{Clock Out Time} - \text{Clock In Time}) - \text{Break Minutes}}{60} \]

Under the US Department of Labor Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), bona fide meal periods (typically 30 minutes or more where the employee is completely relieved from duty) are not work time and are unpaid.

2. Overtime Calculation Rules

  • Standard FLSA (Weekly 40): Covered non-exempt employees receive overtime pay for hours worked over 40 in a workweek at a rate not less than 1.5 times their regular rate of pay.
  • Daily Overtime (8 Hours/Day): Applied in jurisdictions like California, Alaska, and Nevada, where any work beyond 8 hours in a single 24-hour workday qualifies as daily overtime regardless of weekly total.
