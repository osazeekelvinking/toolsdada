Using the Online Scientific Calculator

A scientific calculator provides essential trigonometric, logarithmic, and exponential operations for STEM students, researchers, and engineers.

Trigonometry and Angle Units

By default, the calculator operates in Degrees (DEG). To switch to Radians (RAD) for calculus or wave mechanics, click the DEG/RAD toggle button at the top-left of the keypad.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator support radians and degrees?

Yes! Use the DEG / RAD toggle button to switch trigonometry modes instantly.

Can I use my physical computer keyboard?

Yes, standard numbers (0-9), operators (+, -, *, /), parentheses, Enter (=), and Backspace are supported directly.
